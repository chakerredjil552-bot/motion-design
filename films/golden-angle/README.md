# 137.5° — the golden angle

A 90-second motion film about the "perfect rotation": the **golden angle** (≈137.5°), the turn plants make between each new seed or leaf. Science calls the resulting arrangement **phyllotaxis**. The film covers what the angle is, why it beats every other angle, where it grows in nature, and what engineers build with it. Every frame and every sound is generated by code.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 90 s, 16:9, 60 fps |
| Output | 1920×1080 by default; `--scale 2` gives 3840×2160 |
| Palette | near-black, gold `#FFBE46`, green `#46FFAA`, amber, rose, cyan |
| Camera | dolly zooms, barrel rolls, crane dives, helical orbits, infinite zoom, whip pans with real motion blur |
| Sound | synthesized 120 BPM score (Fibonacci melody), Shepard tone, drums and cue-synced SFX |

## Story

| Time | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 s | Hook | An arm clicks through random angles and asks `θ = ?` |
| 6–21 s | The experiment | Rotate, drop a seed, repeat. 90°, 120° and 144° give spokes and gaps, and 137° nearly works. **137.5077°** snaps into a perfect sunflower, petals bloom, and the camera dives through the seeds. |
| 21–32 s | The name | A dolly zoom on a circle split into 137.5° and 222.5° (their ratio is φ), the **THE GOLDEN ANGLE** title slams in through a barrel roll, then `360° ÷ φ² = 137.5077…°`. |
| 32–44 s | Fibonacci | 21 clockwise and 34 counter-clockwise spirals counted live, then golden squares with Fibonacci numbers and an endless zoom into the golden spiral. |
| 44–66 s | Nature: phyllotaxis | Sunflower (low drone skim), pine cone (helical orbit), spiral aloe (vertigo descent), romanesco (fractal dive into one bud), and leaves on a stem catching sunlight. |
| 66–80 s | We copied it | A solar mirror field on a Fermat spiral, a Fibonacci sphere (random clumps to even points), and a sunflower antenna array focusing a beam. |
| 80–90 s | Finale | Strobe flashback, then one seed per 137.5° turn regrows the flower, ending on the 137.5° end card. |

## Files

- `timeline.js` holds every cue time. The picture (`shots.js`) and the sound (`audio.py`) both read it, so hits stay in sync.
- `core.js` is the engine, adapted from `films/pi`. It adds a camera that can look straight down, impact shake, chromatic-aberration kicks, and per-frame motion blur.
- `shots.js` contains the seven sections. Every frame is a pure function of `t`.
- `audio.py` is the score and sound design (numpy + scipy).
- `render.js` uses parallel headless Chromium workers to render, then encodes with ffmpeg. By default it renders whip-pan frames with 10 sub-frames of motion blur.
- `index.html` is the live preview player.
- `fonts/` holds Inter, Inter Display Black and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License).

## Build

```bash
pip install numpy scipy
python3 audio.py ../../outputs/golden_audio.wav
node render.js --workers 4 --out ../../outputs/golden_angle_1080p60.mp4 --audio ../../outputs/golden_audio.wav
```
